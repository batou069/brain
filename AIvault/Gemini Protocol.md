  2. Visit a Starting Point


  Once you have a browser, you need somewhere to go. You can't use Google to search Geminispace. Instead, you use search
  engines and directories that exist within Geminispace.


  Here are some essential starting URLs. Just copy and paste one of these into your new Gemini browser:


   * `gemini://gemini.circumlunar.space/` - The official Gemini project page. A must-read.
   * `gemini://capcom.circumlunar.space/` - A directory of other Gemini capsules, a great place to start exploring.
   * `gemini://gus.circumlunar.space/` - The "Gemini Universal Search" engine.

  3. Find Content & Explore


  Discovery in Geminispace is more deliberate than on the web. It feels like the early internet.


   * Search Engines: Use search engines like GUS (gemini://gus.circumlunar.space/) or Geminispace.info
     (gemini://geminispace.info/).
   * Directories & Aggregators: Many capsules act as directories or link lists, curating content they find interesting. A
     popular one is Medusae (gemini://medusae.space/).
   * "Gemlogs" (Blogs): A lot of content is in the form of personal blogs, called "gemlogs". People write about technology,
     hobbies, and daily life. It's a much more personal and less commercial experience than the modern web.


  It's a different, slower, more intentional experience. Have fun exploring
